## BussMetrics — National Portal for Business Economics

A static, single-page portal UI in the style of a national government homepage
(deep-navy header, white content panels, red accent blocks, dark-navy footer),
rebuilt for the business-economics domain.

- **Live locally:** `python3 -m http.server 8000` from the repository root, then open `http://localhost:8000`.
- **Files:** `index.html` (markup, 12 sections), `styles.css` (design system), `app.js`
  (scheme search, eligibility checker, news tabs, carousel, counters, accessibility tools),
  `assets/` (logo + favicon SVG, optimised photography, generated QR code), `tools/make_qr.py`
  (dependency-free QR encoder used for the footer code).
- **Rules honoured:** real photography only (no illustrations or emoji), topic-specific
  favicon (rising bar chart mark), section-by-section layout cloned from the reference portal.
